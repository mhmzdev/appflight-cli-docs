Creates an AppFlight account without the mobile app.

```bash
appflight signup
```

```
AppFlight Terms & Conditions: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/…
AppFlight Privacy Policy: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/…
Do you accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy? [y/N] y
Full name: Ada Lovelace
Email: ada@example.com
Password:
Confirm password:
Creating your account…
✓ Account created for ada@example.com.
  Check your inbox to verify your email, then run `appflight login`.
```

Individual accounts only — organizations are onboarded in the app.

## Flags

| Flag | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `--accept-terms` | Accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy without the interactive prompt. For scripted sign-ups. |
| `--resend <email>` | Resend the verification email for an account that already signed up. |

## What it does, and does not do

`signup` creates the account and sends the verification email. It does **not** log you in: no API key
is minted and no credentials file is written. The next step is always
[`appflight login`](/commands/login).

Email verification is required before the CLI can mint a key, so an unverified account cannot upload
yet. `login` will tell you if you try.

## Terms and privacy

Terms come **first**, before you are asked for anything else. Both URLs are printed and an explicit
`y` is required; anything else — including pressing enter — prints `Cancelled.` and exits without
creating anything. That ordering is deliberate: the account is created by the call that happens
*after* this prompt, so declining can never leave a half-made account behind.

For non-interactive use, `--accept-terms` records the same acceptance:

```bash
appflight signup --accept-terms
```

## Resending the verification email

```bash
appflight signup --resend ada@example.com
```

> **Note:** `--resend` also prompts for your password. Sending a verification link requires a fresh sign-in, not just the address.

## When signup fails

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| `That email is already registered. Run` `appflight login` `instead.` | The account exists — log in. |
| `This email belongs to an organization. Sign in through the AppFlight app.` | Org members are onboarded and sign in via the app. |
| A weak-password refusal from the auth service | Choose a longer password. |

More detail in [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
