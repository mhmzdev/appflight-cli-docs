The AppFlight app is how **testers** receive your builds. As a **developer** you can do almost
everything from the terminal — see [Quick Start](/quick-start) — and only need the app for two things.

## Download

<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.mhmz.app_flight" target="_blank">
  **Get it on Google Play →**
</a>

Available on Android.

## Invited as a tester?

Install the app, open it, and choose **Continue as a tester** — you do not need to create an app or an
organization. Sign in with the email address you were invited on, and the apps you have been invited
to are already there. From then on you get a push notification every time a new build is uploaded.

Invited on an address that had no account yet? That is fine — the invite is held and attaches itself
the moment you sign up with that email.

## What developers still need it for

| Genuinely app-only | Why |
|---|---|
| Subscribing to First Class | Billed through Google Play, so the purchase happens on a device signed in to the account being billed. Run [`appflight upgrade`](/commands/upgrade) to get the link. |
| Changing an organization member's role | Admin action under **Settings → Team**. |

Everything else has a terminal equivalent:

| Used to need the app | Now |
|---|---|
| Sign up | [`appflight signup`](/commands/signup) |
| Generate an API key | [`appflight keys create`](/commands/keys) |
| Create an app | [`appflight apps create`](/commands/apps) |
| See your apps and plan usage | [`appflight apps list`](/commands/apps) |
| Revoke a key | [`appflight keys revoke`](/commands/keys) or [`appflight logout`](/commands/logout) |

## What the app is still good at

- Reviewing builds — APK metadata, version history, release notes
- Inviting testers by email and managing access per app
- Push notifications when a build lands
- Checking or changing your subscription

## Requirements

- Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher
- An AppFlight account — create one in the app, or with [`appflight signup`](/commands/signup)
